Changes include head proportion, surface, pose, game rig fitting and assembly.
These head components retain their own attribution, source URL and source-file
SHA256 in the GLB node extras. Body, rig and original Peris equipment retain
their separate CC BY-SA 3.0 attribution.

Editable sources are retained locally under assets/source/battle/ in immutable
edition folders. assets/manifest.json identifies each active editable source;
public/models/battle/faction-rosters.json records its source edition and hashes.
The original roster-v3 sources remain available for comparison.
Source inventory and hashes: assets/references/units/0ad/inventory-roster-v3.json
Source family catalog: assets/references/units/roster-sources-v3.json
Rebuild prerequisites: installed project npm dependencies; Blender 4.5 with COLLADA import support; Python 3 with Pillow.
Rebuild order: scripts/fetch-roster-references.mjs; scripts/convert-reference-textures.py --inventory inventory-roster-v3.json; scripts/blender/cache_reference_imports.py (Blender) -- --inventory inventory-roster-v3.json; scripts/blender/build_faction_rosters.py (Blender); scripts/prepare-faction-rosters.mjs.
Use PERIS_ROSTER_EDITION to identify the matching editable-source edition when preparing exports. Earlier sources and runtime packs are retained.

These are visual prototypes. Seven troop looks use existing infantry/archer/cavalry battle rules. The ram and catapult/stonehurler roles are inspection studies. Academy research and Workshop recruitment remain design plans.
