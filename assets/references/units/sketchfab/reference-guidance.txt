ORC
Viewed: NenZ Orc Tanker (front, shield side, rear three-quarter, back), and
marcosoresina Test Orc Head (face closeup, profile, both three-quarters, rear low).
Build a relatively small head into a huge trapezius/deltoid frame, long heavy arms
and short bowed legs. The bust demonstrates jaw projection, thick lower lip,
recessed small eyes, nested brow/cheek folds and a cupped attached ear. Preserve
our low square face/topknot and small upward lower-jaw tusks; do not copy the
bust's tall bald dome, aged round chin, or add downward cheek fangs.
Our kit is copper-edged charcoal overlapping plate over fitted leather/hide,
with cross straps, orange ragged cloth, exposed upper arms and believable
rivets/grips. Tanker's wood shield/hammer/fur explain construction and materials,
but our line retains its portrait axe and angular shield. Keep pike, bow and
elite halberd silhouettes distinct. Wolf scout, horse light, mammoth heavy.
Giant idle holds its boulder overhead with real two-hand contact.

ELF
Viewed: michael_grodkowski Elf Warrior (all six stored body/armor views), and
Mark Wilson Head Sculpts (four comparative face/ear views).
The body demonstrates narrow long limbs, flowing fluted plate, overlapping
waist lames and a continuous flexible dark layer, with mail-like flex zones at
elbows/hips. Translate to silver-edged green leaf plates, woven green cloth and
dark leather. Preserve support and movement gaps, not holes between floating
plates. Closed gold helmet/A-pose provide construction only; they do not prove
bare ears, combat grips or the final pose.
The head study has attached thick cupped ears and narrow cheek/jaw planes.
Reduce its exaggerated ear length, brows/lashes and feminine pointed chin to
our restrained male portraits. Keep blond hair/open face, fine sword, leaf pike,
wood bow and sweeping elite polearm. Stag scout, horse light, elk heavy.
Rootstone must be structural roots/rock, not gray human cloth; idle cradles stone
at chest. Comparative heads are CC BY-NC; commercial asset reuse is not cleared.

DWARF
Viewed: Gabriele_Zero3DArt Dwarf Warrior Game Ready (five stored body/face views).
Real stout mass, thick upper arms/short legs, deep brow/broad nose and beard roots
matter more than shrinking a human. Beard has separate directional braids and
flyaways; our portrait requires warm russet braids/copper cuffs, not its dark-gray
beard or helmetless mohawk. Layer angular iron/copper plates over flexible dark
cloth/leather, with hip mail only where compatible; armor rims, cape folds and
hammer wraps must have depth and contact.
Source pose grips hammer shaft in one hand and cups head with the other. Our line
uses one-hand hammer plus shield, so choose appropriate weapon weight/scale
instead of copying the giant display hammer. Keep tall pike, horizontal real
crossbow and two-head rune axe distinct. Goat scout, pony light, boar heavy.
Runestone is angular cracked/engraved blocks with structural joints; no gold
smooth donut joints or human clothing. Boulder idle is chest cradle.

UNDEAD
Viewed: Pigcraft Bone Knight (front, side, three-quarter, back).
Skull needs real orbit/nasal cavities, mandible and irregular teeth; body needs
actual rib/spine/pelvis gaps and paired long bones. Layer broken corroded plate
around that frame. Living-troop opaque underclothes must not fill skeletal holes;
use straps/torn violet fragments and retain bone negative space in LOD/shadows.
Transfer aged bone versus metal roughness and thick plate rims, not the source
horns/greatsword onto every role. Keep portrait spear, hooded bow, elite halberd,
bone-horse scout/light and genuine skeletal mammoth heavy. Tomb slab colossus
has inscribed block anatomy and a shoulder/head-raised boulder, not a gray human.

GNOME
Matheus Elias Gnome Character (front/side) is rejected primary: balloon nose,
pointed red garden hat, torso-hiding white beard and mitten hands do not match
our serious short-bearded engineer soldier. Keep only style contrast/silhouette
lessons. PPrager Gnome Warrior (front/side/rear) is visibly green goblin anatomy;
author credits Last Goblin Standing by Prospass. Use dented plate, soft shirt,
mail throat and leather wraps as gear-construction study only. Extended arms and
open fingers are not actual weapon grips or a battle stance.
Gustavo Groppo Gnome (three credited portfolio screenshots) is an offline 3D
render: skin pores/creases, connected cupped ears, fine hair, uneven teeth,
stitches/leather folds, brass wear and carved wood are a useful finish benchmark.
Its old fleshy nose, white beard, grin, lantern/skull staff and shaman kit are not
our troop design, downloadable rig or web performance evidence. Preserve our
moderate face, short brown beard, burgundy soft cap/goggles, quilted leather,
brass fittings, actual sword/pike/crossbow and clockwork elite. Engineer body,
mechanical face/boar/gear mechanisms and animated contacts still need direct
study; research is not complete just because three sources were viewed.

DEMON
Pedro B. Goulart Demon Warrior & Axes of Agony (front/detail/side/rear) shows
continuous organic ridges, attached spines and claw transitions, rough pale
shell against red soft tissue, and a rough ornate axe with sharper metal edge.
Use connected anatomy/material hierarchy principles. Do not copy its single
mouth-eye, pale nude exoskeleton, red seam biology or twin axes into the two-eyed
red Div with curved ridged horns, charcoal/copper crafted plate and red cloth.
CC BY4 is displayed, but download required login; no archive/rig/UVs acquired.
Camilo Aldana Balrog (side/rear/detail) helps broad upper-body mass, bent limbs,
claws and organic wing/back attachment. The file named torso-wing-root-detail crops
most of the head; dark fire lighting hides face/horn/surface detail. These shots
are limited silhouette evidence. Do not copy recognizable LOTR wings/tail/fire
weapon or treat glow as a material-quality benchmark. Our original Div targets
and per-role weapons/cloth remain primary; face/horn roots, animal saddle gait
and real boulder contact remain unresolved. No literal runtime asset approved.
