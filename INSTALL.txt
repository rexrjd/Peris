PERIS — MAGE TOWER & SPELLS

INSTALL
1. Merge the included src/, supabase/, tests/, and package.json into your existing project, replacing matching files. This update builds on the previous city-building-slots update.
2. Existing shared worlds: run supabase/UPGRADE_MAGE_TOWER.sql in the Supabase SQL Editor BEFORE deploying the updated app. If you have not installed the city-building-slots database update yet, run the included supabase/UPGRADE_CITY_SLOTS.sql instead; it now includes the mage tower too. These upgrades preserve existing cities and progress. Do not use a FRESH_INSTALL script on an existing world.
3. Run npm run build, then push and redeploy through your usual GitHub/Vercel flow.
Solo campaign saves continue automatically; no database update is needed for solo play.

PLAY
Choose an empty city plot and build a Mage tower. The tower is optional, can occupy any unlocked regular plot, and is limited to one per city. It has ten upgrade levels with a different visual at every level. Each level unlocks two spells.
Select the completed tower and open its spellbook. Research each spell once by paying the displayed resources. Higher-level pages can be viewed before upgrading, but their research remains locked.
During combat, open Spellbook in the battle command panel, choose a learned spell and its target, and cast. Mana resets each battle: 20 + 10 per tower level. Casting has an eight-second battle-time cooldown. Damage, healing, morale, speed, protection, and resurrection effects work in solo and shared-world combat. Persistent buffs last that battle; attack buffs cap at +50%, protection at 40%, and speed at +75%. Healing and resurrection cannot exceed a formation's starting troop count.
