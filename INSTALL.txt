PERIS — BUILDING SLOTS AND LIVING CITY

1. Copy the included src, supabase and tests folders plus package.json into your project root. Merge folders and replace matching files. All city renderer dependencies are included; this supersedes the earlier city graphics/walls patches.
2. For an existing shared world, run supabase/UPGRADE_CITY_SLOTS.sql in the Supabase SQL editor BEFORE deploying the client. It preserves your world and migrates existing barracks, stables and combined storehouses. Do not run a fresh-install script on an existing world.
3. Run npm run build, commit/push and redeploy. Solo games need only the updated client; their saves migrate automatically.

FEATURES
- Six empty city plots initially. Main-building upgrades unlock two more plots per level, up to sixteen, and enlarge the city and its surrounding walls.
- Select a plot to choose barracks, stables, smithy, warehouse or granary. Any of these can be built multiple times and independently upgraded to level five.
- The main building, walls and resource sites remain unique. Timber, stone, fields and the dedicated riverside fishing site are outside the walls.
- Each smithy level adds 4% army damage, capped at 60% across all smithies. This applies to campaign battles in solo and shared worlds.
- Warehouses add 2,500 timber/stone/gold capacity per level; granaries add 2,500 food capacity per level. Multiple buildings stack.
- Fishing adds 8 food per minute per level and is only buildable at its river site.
- Walking villagers carry supplies along city roads and through the gate. Reduced-motion settings stop their movement.
